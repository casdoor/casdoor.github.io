Intégrations disponibles

Le Marketplace contient 48 intégrations : 13 intégrations d'applications, 5 fournisseurs d'identité, 6 actions et 24 thèmes de connexion. Installez-les dans la console sous Identity → Marketplace, ou ouvrez-en une ci-dessous pour lire son guide d'installation sur marketplace.casdoor.com.

Les intégrations marquées Commercial ou Enterprise nécessitent une licence auto-hébergée de cette édition, voir tarifs auto-hébergés. Toutes les autres sont open source dans casdoor/casdoor-integrations et gratuites dans toutes les éditions.

Intégrations d'applications​

L'installation crée une application Casdoor avec les URL de redirection, les types d'autorisation et le format de jeton dont l'application a besoin, puis affiche les paramètres propres à l'application avec votre adresse Casdoor, l'ID client et le secret client préremplis.

Argo CDConnect Argo CD to Casdoor over OpenID Connect: oidc.config in argocd-cm, the client secret, PKCE, CLI login and RBAC from Casdoor groups.GiteaAdd Casdoor to Gitea as an OpenID Connect authentication source: the add-oauth command, callback URL, and admins from a Casdoor group.GitLabConfigure self-managed GitLab to sign users in with Casdoor over OpenID Connect: gitlab.rb provider settings, account creation and group-based admins.GrafanaSet up Grafana single sign-on with Casdoor over OpenID Connect: Generic OAuth settings, PKCE, and Grafana roles mapped from Casdoor groups.ImmichLet people sign in to Immich with Casdoor: OAuth settings in the Immich admin, the web and mobile redirect URIs, and storage label mapping.NextcloudSet up Nextcloud single sign-on with Casdoor using the OpenID Connect user backend (user_oidc): provider command, user ID and group mapping.OOpen WebUISet up Open WebUI single sign-on with Casdoor over OpenID Connect: OAuth environment variables, sign-up, and group sync from Casdoor groups.PortainerSet up Portainer single sign-on with Casdoor using Portainer's custom OAuth provider: endpoints, user identifier, scopes and automatic user creation.Proxmox VEAdd Casdoor to Proxmox VE as an OpenID Connect realm: the pveum command, redirect URL, usernames, groups and permissions.VaultwardenTurn on Vaultwarden single sign-on with Casdoor over OpenID Connect: the SSO_* settings, the redirect URI, and the email verification check.DeepSeek HarnessCommercialSign DeepSeek Harness in to Casdoor as an AI agent and give it the MCP servers of your organization through the Casdoor MCP gateway, with tool permissions, call records and audit, all set up from the web pages of Casdoor and DeepSeek Harness.HigressCommercialPut Casdoor Fournisseurs d'identité​

L'installation crée un fournisseur permettant aux utilisateurs de se connecter à Casdoor via un système d'identité qu'ils possèdent déjà, et l'ajoute éventuellement à la page de connexion d'une application.

AutheliaLet people sign in to Casdoor through Authelia over OpenID Connect: the provider settings, and the client to add to Authelia's configuration.authentikLet people sign in to Casdoor with their authentik accounts over OpenID Connect: the provider settings, and the application and OAuth2 provider to create in authentik.GitLab Self-ManagedLet people sign in to Casdoor with accounts on your own GitLab over OpenID Connect: the provider settings, and the OAuth application to create in GitLab.KeycloakLet people sign in to Casdoor with their Keycloak accounts over OpenID Connect: the provider settings for a Keycloak realm, and the client to create on the Keycloak side.ZZITADELLet people sign in to Casdoor with their ZITADEL accounts over OpenID Connect: the provider settings, and the web application to create in the ZITADEL Console.

Actions autorisées​

Actions are code that runs inside Casdoor as sandboxed WebAssembly and can reach only the hosts listed in their manifest: payment and SMS gateways, password checks, password migration from another system, migrations from Auth0 and Keycloak, and sign-in confirmation by push.

RevolutTake card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Revolut Pay payments for Casdoor products and plans through the Revolut Merchant API, on Revolut's hosted checkout page.VonageSend sign-up, sign-in and MFA verification codes by SMS through the Vonage SMS API, formerly Nexmo.Common password checkCommercialRefuse the most used passwords and passwords that contain the username, with a list built into the action; nothing leaves Casdoor.Feishu / Lark sign-in confirmationCommercialWhen a sign-in needs the second factor, send the user a Feishu or Lark card; one tap on Confirm signs them in, no code to type.Have I Been Pwned password checkCommercialRefuse passwords that have appeared in data breaches, and ask users to change one at sign-in, checked against Have I Been Pwned without sending the password.Password migration by HTTPCommercialMove the passwords of users imported without one: on the first sign-in Casdoor asks your old system over HTTPS whether the password is right, then keeps it.

Thèmes de connexion​

L'installation d'un thème modifie l'apparence des pages de connexion d'une application. La boîte de dialogue d'installation propose un aperçu sur la vraie page de connexion de cette application, et la désinstallation restaure l'apparence précédente. Vous pouvez essayer tous les thèmes sur le site de démonstration.

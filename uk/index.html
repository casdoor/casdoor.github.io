Built for every kind of login

One login for every internal app

Connect Grafana, GitLab, Nextcloud and your own applications to Casdoor over OIDC, SAML or CAS. Employees sign in once with their company account and a second factor, and reach every app from there.

Key capabilities

  • OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect provider
  • SAML 2.0 identity provider and CAS server
  • LDAP and RADIUS for legacy systems and VPNs
  • Sync from Active Directory, Okta and Google Workspace
  • TOTP, passkeys and step-up MFA
  • Session management and single sign-out
Connect an application
auth.acme.example.com/applications

Works with the stack you already have

Casdoor speaks OpenID Connect, OAuth 2.0, SAML and CAS, so most frameworks and off-the-shelf apps connect with their existing login settings. For everything else there are SDKs and a REST API.

SDKs: Go, Java, Spring Boot, Node.js, Python, PHP, .NET, Rust, Ruby, React, Vue, Angular, Flutter, iOS, Android, Unity

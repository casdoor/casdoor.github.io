Customize the Login Page in Casdoor: Custom Styling of the Authentication UI Without Forking

2026年11月2日 · 阅读时间：一分钟
Yang Luo
Casdoor Maintainer

Yes, Casdoor supports custom styling of its authentication UI, and you don't need to fork the frontend to get it. Every application has a UI Customization tab where you set the logo, favicon, background image, custom CSS for desktop and mobile, the position of the form, a side panel, footer HTML and a theme. Two tables, Signin items and Signup items, decide which fields the login and sign-up pages show, in which order, with which labels and placeholders. All of it is stored per application in the database, so one Casdoor instance can serve many differently branded login pages, and organization admins can edit their own pages within safe limits.

Every tool in a typical self-hosted DevOps stack can act as an OpenID Connect client: Gitea and Argo CD speak OIDC natively, Jenkins does through the oic-auth plugin, Grafana and Portainer through generic OAuth 2.0, and MinIO, Harbor and GitLab through their OIDC settings. Point all of them at one Casdoor instance, give each tool its own Casdoor application, and put your people in Casdoor groups. Each tool then reads the groups claim and decides who is an administrator. This post lists the callback URL, the config keys and the group mapping for each tool, then covers the parts they share.

DingTalk, WeCom, Lark, Infoflow and WeChat OAuth Login with Casdoor

2026年11月2日 · 阅读时间：一分钟
Yang Luo
Casdoor Maintainer

Casdoor has built-in OAuth providers for DingTalk, WeCom (WeChat Work), Lark (Feishu), Baidu Infoflow and WeChat. You create an app in each vendor's developer console, paste its credentials into a Casdoor provider, and add the provider to your application. Casdoor runs the vendor flow (QR scan on a desktop, or sign-in inside the vendor's own client), fetches the corporate access token where the vendor needs one, and maps the vendor's user ID to a Casdoor user. Your application only talks OIDC to Casdoor and never sees a DingTalk header, a WeCom user_ticket or a WeChat unionid. For DingTalk, WeCom and Lark, a syncer can also import the whole org directory ahead of the first sign-in.

Nginx CAS 认证与 SSO：使用 Casdoor 在 Nginx 后保护任意应用

2026年11月2日 · 阅读时间：一分钟
Yang Luo
Casdoor Maintainer

开源版 nginx 没有 CAS 模块，因此实际上“nginx CAS 认证”意味着两种配置之一。要么 nginx 通过其 auth_request 模块向每个请求询问认证服务，由 Casdoor 回答（直接通过 /api/forward-auth，或通过 casdoor-forward-auth 或 OAuth2 Proxy），要么 nginx 后面的应用本身是一个 CAS 客户端，并与 Casdoor 的 CAS 服务器端点通信。如果您可以重新构建 nginx，Casdoor nginx 模块会在 nginx 内部运行登录；如果您不想运行 nginx，Casdoor 的站点代理可以位于应用之前。在所有情况下，登录页面都是 Casdoor 的，因此一个 SSO 会话、一组权限和一个 MFA 策略覆盖所有应用。

RP-Initiated Logout and SSO Logout in OpenID Connect: How Casdoor Signs Users Out

2026年11月2日 · 阅读时间：一分钟
Yang Luo
Casdoor Maintainer

Logging a user out of an OpenID Connect app takes more than deleting your own session cookie. The user is still signed in at the identity provider, so the next "Sign in" click goes straight through without a password, and the other apps that share that identity provider session stay signed in too. A proper logout has three parts: clear your app's local session, send the browser to the provider's end_session_endpoint (RP-initiated logout) so the provider session ends, and let the provider tell the other apps through back-channel logout. With Casdoor, the end-session endpoint is /api/logout, back-channel logout is configured per application, and refresh tokens can be revoked at /api/login/oauth/revoke.

Steam OAuth and Telegram OAuth with Casdoor: Adding Non-Standard Social Logins Through OIDC

2026年11月2日 · 阅读时间：一分钟
Yang Luo
Casdoor Maintainer

There is no "Steam OAuth": Steam signs users in with OpenID 2.0 and gives you a 64-bit SteamID, nothing more. Telegram's classic Login Widget isn't OAuth either: it hands your page a set of user fields plus an HMAC-SHA256 hash that you check with your bot token. Telegram now also runs a real OpenID Connect provider at oauth.telegram.org. If you put Casdoor between your app and these providers, Casdoor does the protocol-specific checks and your app only ever speaks OpenID Connect to Casdoor, the same way it would for Google or GitHub.
